About the Contest

Inspired by a life that changed the world

Sreshta Contest is a value-based educational initiative by ISKCON Abids, Hyderabad, inspired by the life and teachings of His Divine Grace Srila Prabhupada — the founder-acharya of ISKCON.

At the age of 69, with almost no money and no institutional support, Srila Prabhupada journeyed alone from India to America. What happened next became one of the most remarkable success stories of modern times.

"Whatever action a great person performs, common people follow."
— Bhagavad-gita 3.21

Through determination, wisdom, compassion, and unwavering faith, he inspired millions of people and established a global movement dedicated to spiritual wisdom, character building, and service to humanity.

Get involved

Join as a volunteer

Volunteering for this contest is a wonderful opportunity to serve Srila Prabhupada directly — through your time, your energy, and your devotion. No special qualification is needed; only an eager heart.

There are many ways you can help:

  • Outreach & promotion — spread the word in schools, temples, and local communities
  • Registration coordination — help contestants register and guide them through the process
  • Digital & social media — create content, manage updates, and amplify the contest online

Volunteering with us is also an opportunity to deepen your own understanding of Srila Prabhupada's incredible journey — from a small printing press in New York to a worldwide spiritual movement. Come, be a part of this celebration.
