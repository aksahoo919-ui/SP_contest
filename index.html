About the Contest

Explore Values that shaped Leaders from Centuries

Dear Parents,

It's well known fact that children today are continuously bombarded with immoral inputs coming from internet, TV, Smart phones, Magazines and what not. No one of us has capacity to guard them against these inputs 24hrs a day. Divine values of individual not only stay with him 24hrs a day but also safeguard him from making wrong choices in life specially when no one is around.

"Whatever action a great person performs, common people follow."
— Bhagavad-gita 3.21

Support with a donation

Your support helps us put the Bhagavad Gita and the value-education booklet into the hands of many more children — so they grow in Character, Competence and Culture.

Get involved

Join as a volunteer

Volunteering for the Value Education Contest is a wonderful opportunity to serve Srila Prabhupada — by helping children carry values that will guide them all their lives. No special qualification is needed; only an eager heart.

There are many ways you can help:

  • Schools & kit distribution — introduce the contest in schools and help get the kits to students
  • WhatsApp group coordination — help students join the right group and keep them informed
  • Answer submission support — guide students through the online submission, 16th–21st January 2027
  • Digital & social media — create content, share updates and spread the word online

Volunteering with us is also a chance to grow in the very values we share with the children — Character, Competence and Culture. Come, be a part of this effort to reach students across 450+ districts.
